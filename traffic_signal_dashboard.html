Analyst Key Takeaways

💡 Why Static Signals Fail
Fixed 120s timer controllers force empty lanes to hold green signals while congested lanes accumulate compound queue delay, causing exponential wait spikes up to 45 seconds.
🚀 RL Controller Advantage
The Deep Q / PPO Reinforcement Learning model evaluates live YOLO queue densities every second, allocating dynamic green waves that clear queues before bottlenecks compound.
